Solid Spaghetti Casserole

A hearty vegetarian spaghetti casserole with impossible beef, cream cheese, sour cream, and mozzarella.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 6 tbsp salted butter
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 jars spaghetti sauce
  • 10 oz angel hair pasta
  • 6 oz cream cheese
  • 12 oz sour cream
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • Impossible beef

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F and boil pasta al dente.
  2. Cook onion in avocado oil until browned, then add garlic.
  3. Brown impossible beef with Italian seasoning.
  4. Mix cream cheese, sour cream, mozzarella, and seasonings.
  5. Layer noodles, sauce, cream mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella in a casserole dish.
  6. Bake 15–20 minutes until cheese is melted.
