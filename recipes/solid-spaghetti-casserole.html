Solid Spaghetti Casserole

A hearty vegetarian spaghetti casserole with impossible beef, cream cheese, sour cream, and mozzarella.

Ingredients

Sauce and Pasta

  • 2 (24 oz) jars spaghetti sauce
  • 10 oz angel hair pasta
  • 6 tbsp salted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil

Vegetables and Protein

  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 large onion
  • Impossible beef

Seasoning and Cheese

  • 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 6 oz cream cheese or Neufchâtel
  • 12 oz sour cream
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella

Instructions

1

Prepare

Preheat oven to 400°F. Boil pasta until al dente, then drain.

2

Cook the aromatics

Cook onion in avocado oil over medium heat until browned. Add garlic and stir about 1 minute.

3

Cook the protein

Add impossible beef and Italian seasoning. Cook until browned and no pink remains.

4

Mix the creamy layer

In a bowl, mix cream cheese, sour cream, part of the mozzarella, and seasoning until combined.

5

Layer

Butter a casserole dish. Layer noodles, sauce, cream mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.

6

Bake

Bake 15–20 minutes, until cheese is melted and the casserole is hot.

Notes

  • This is a heavy, rich casserole. Let it sit a few minutes before cutting.
