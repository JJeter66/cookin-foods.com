Solid Spaghetti Casserole

A hearty vegetarian spaghetti casserole with impossible beef, cream cheese, sour cream, and mozzarella.

Ingredients

Sauce and Pasta

  • 2 (24 oz) jars spaghetti sauce
  • 10 oz angel hair pasta
  • 6 tbsp salted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil

Vegetables and Protein

  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 large onion
  • Impossible beef

Seasoning and Cheese

  • 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 6 oz cream cheese or Neufchâtel
  • 12 oz sour cream
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella

Instructions

1

Prepare

Preheat oven to 400°F. Boil angel hair until al dente. Drain, return noodles to the pot, add 1 jar of spaghetti sauce, mix well, and let it sit.

2

Cook the aromatics

Finely chop the onion and garlic. Heat avocado oil over medium heat and cook onion until browned, about 10 minutes. Add garlic and stir about 1 minute.

3

Cook the protein

Make a well in the onion mixture, add Impossible beef, and season with 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Break it up and cook until no pink remains, then stir it into the aromatics.

4

Mix the creamy layer

In a bowl, mix cream cheese or Neufchâtel, sour cream, 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, and 2 cups mozzarella until combined.

5

Layer

Add 4 tbsp butter to the bottom of a 9×13 casserole dish in small pieces. Layer half the sauced noodles, half the meat sauce, and the cream cheese mixture. Add the remaining noodles and meat sauce, then dot with the remaining butter and top with the remaining mozzarella.

6

Bake

Bake 15–20 minutes, until the cheese is melted and browned to your liking. Let sit a few minutes before cutting. Garnish with parsley if desired.

Notes

  • This is a heavy, rich casserole. Let it sit a few minutes before cutting.
