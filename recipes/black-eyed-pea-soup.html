Black-Eyed Pea Soup

Instant Pot black-eyed pea soup with liquid smoke, coconut aminos, collards, and smoked paprika.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 medium yellow or white onion, diced
  • 1 medium red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ cups dry black-eyed peas
  • 3½ cups vegetable broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 tsp liquid smoke
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 small bunch collard greens, chopped

Instructions

1

Sauté

Use Instant Pot sauté mode. Cook onion 4–5 minutes, then add bell pepper and garlic for 1 minute.

2

Pressure cook

Add remaining ingredients except collard greens. Secure lid and pressure cook on HIGH for 17 minutes.

3

Release

Let pressure release naturally 15 minutes, then release remaining pressure.

4

Finish

Remove bay leaves. Stir in collard greens and adjust seasoning.

5

Serve

Serve with rice, cornbread, or baked sweet potatoes.

Notes

  • Liquid smoke gives the classic smoky note. Flavor deepens overnight.
