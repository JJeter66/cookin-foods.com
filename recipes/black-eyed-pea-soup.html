Black-Eyed Pea Soup

Instant Pot black-eyed pea soup with liquid smoke, coconut aminos, collards, and smoked paprika.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Olive or avocado oil
  • Sweet onion
  • Red bell pepper
  • Garlic
  • Dry black-eyed peas
  • Vegetable broth
  • Bay leaves
  • Smoked paprika
  • Cayenne
  • Coconut aminos
  • Liquid smoke
  • Sea salt
  • Black pepper
  • Collard greens

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion in Instant Pot, then add bell pepper and garlic.
  2. Add remaining ingredients except collard greens.
  3. Pressure cook on HIGH for 17 minutes.
  4. Natural release 15 minutes, then release remaining pressure.
  5. Remove bay leaves, stir, season, and serve.
