Black-Eyed Pea Soup

Instant Pot black-eyed pea soup with liquid smoke, coconut aminos, collards, and smoked paprika.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 medium yellow or white onion, diced
  • 1 medium red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ cups dry black-eyed peas
  • 3½ cups vegetable broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 tsp liquid smoke
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 small bunch collard greens, chopped

Instructions

1

Sauté aromatics

Use Instant Pot sauté mode. Cook onion in oil 4–5 minutes until translucent, then add bell pepper and garlic and sauté 1 minute. Press Cancel.

2

Pressure cook

Add remaining ingredients except collard greens. Stir to combine, secure the lid, and pressure cook on HIGH for 17 minutes.

3

Natural release

Allow 15 minutes of natural release, then manually release remaining pressure.

4

Finish greens

Remove bay leaves. Stir in collard greens and adjust seasoning to taste.

5

Serve

Serve with white or brown rice, cornbread, or roasted/baked sweet potatoes.

Notes

  • Liquid smoke gives the classic smoky bacon-style note; do not skip it if you want that flavor.
  • Flavor deepens overnight.
  • Freeze in individual portions for easy meals.
