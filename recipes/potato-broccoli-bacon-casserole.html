Potato Broccoli Bacon Casserole

A loaded potato, broccoli, cheddar, and plant-based bacon casserole.

Ingredients

Casserole

  • 3–4 large baking potatoes
  • 5 cups fresh broccoli florets, chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1⅔ cups sour cream
  • 1 package Morning Star bacon, cooked and chopped
  • 1 bunch green onion, sliced
  • 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese, freshly shredded

Instructions

1

Bake the potatoes

Preheat oven to 375°F. Wash potatoes, pierce with a fork, wrap in foil, and bake about 1 hour until tender.

2

Cook the broccoli

About 10 minutes before the potatoes are done, boil broccoli for 5 minutes and drain well.

3

Build the casserole

Remove potatoes from foil and place in a large bowl. Coarsely chop with skins on. Stir in butter, salt, pepper, sour cream, bacon, green onion, broccoli, and cheddar.

4

Bake

Spoon into a greased casserole dish, cover with foil, and bake 15 minutes until hot and melted.

Notes

  • Add extra sour cream or a splash of milk for a creamier texture.
