Potato Broccoli Bacon Casserole

A loaded potato, broccoli, cheddar, and plant-based bacon casserole.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 3–4 large baking potatoes
  • 5 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 2/3 cups sour cream
  • 1 package morning star bacon
  • Green onion
  • 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Bake potatoes at 375°F for about 1 hour.
  2. Boil broccoli for 5 minutes and drain.
  3. Chop baked potatoes with skins on in a large bowl.
  4. Stir in butter, salt, pepper, sour cream, bacon, chives, broccoli, and cheddar.
  5. Bake covered for 15 minutes until heated through and cheese is melted.
