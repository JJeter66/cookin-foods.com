Impossible Sloppy Joes

Plant-based sloppy joes with peppers, onions, garlic, tomato sauce, and warm spices.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegan Worcestershire sauce
  • Impossible meat
  • Sugar
  • Chili powder
  • Cumin
  • Smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Dice onion, garlic, and bell pepper.
  2. Cook vegetables in oil until browned and sticky.
  3. Add garlic briefly, then tomato sauce, Worcestershire, sugar, chili powder, cumin, and paprika.
  4. Add impossible meat and cook until browned.
  5. Serve on toasted buns.
