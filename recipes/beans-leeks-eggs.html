Beans, Leeks & Eggs

Soft leeks and white beans with jammy eggs and miso vinaigrette.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Leeks
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Garlic cloves
  • Fresh thyme
  • Cannellini or butter beans
  • Eggs
  • Tarragon or parsley
  • Toast
  • White miso paste
  • Dijon mustard
  • Garlic clove
  • Red wine vinegar

Instructions

  1. Clean leeks thoroughly.
  2. Cook leeks with olive oil until soft and jammy.
  3. Boil eggs 6 minutes for jammy centers, then ice bath.
  4. Whisk miso vinaigrette.
  5. Fold white beans into the leeks and warm through.
  6. Serve with eggs, herbs, vinaigrette, and toast.
