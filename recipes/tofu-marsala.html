Tofu Marsala

Golden tofu cutlets in a mushroom Marsala sauce over mashed potatoes or pasta.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Poultry seasoning
  • Super-firm tofu
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Shallot
  • Cremini mushrooms
  • Fresh thyme
  • Marsala wine
  • Cooking sherry
  • Parsley

Instructions

  1. Whisk flour and seasonings for dredge.
  2. Slice tofu into cutlets and coat in flour mixture.
  3. Pan-fry tofu in butter and oil until golden.
  4. Cook garlic, shallot, mushrooms, and thyme.
  5. Add Marsala and sherry and reduce to a glossy sauce.
  6. Season, garnish with parsley, and serve over mashed potatoes or pasta.
