Tofu Marsala

Golden tofu cutlets in a mushroom Marsala sauce over mashed potatoes or pasta.

Ingredients

Tofu and Dredge

  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp poultry seasoning
  • 1 block super-firm tofu

Pan and Sauce

  • ½ stick butter or dairy-free butter
  • Olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ shallot, minced
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tsp fresh thyme
  • ¾ cup Marsala wine
  • ¼ cup cooking sherry

Serve With

  • Mashed potatoes or noodles
  • Fresh parsley

Instructions

1

Create the dredge

In a wide bowl or plate, whisk flour, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and poultry seasoning until uniform.

2

Slice and coat tofu

Slice super-firm tofu into ¼-inch cutlets. Press each cutlet into the flour mixture, flip, coat again, shake off excess, and line them up on a plate.

3

Fry tofu

Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add butter and a drizzle of olive oil. Fry tofu 3–5 minutes per side until golden brown and crisp, adjusting heat if it browns too fast. Set tofu on paper towels to rest.

4

Make the Marsala sauce

In the same skillet, cook garlic, shallot, mushrooms, and thyme. Add Marsala wine and cooking sherry. Simmer and reduce 7–10 minutes, or longer if needed, until it looks like a glossy brown gravy.

5

Adjust seasoning

Taste and add salt and pepper carefully. Do not over-salt.

6

Serve

Add tofu back to the pan or spoon sauce over tofu. Serve immediately over mashed potatoes or fresh pasta and finish with parsley.

Notes

  • Super-firm tofu works best for crisping.
  • More shallots never hurt anyone.
  • Mashed potatoes are the preferred base for this one.
