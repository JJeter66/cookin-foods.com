General Tso's Tofu

Crispy tofu and broccoli tossed in a sticky, sweet, spicy General Tso-style sauce.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Canola oil
  • Broccoli
  • Fresh ginger
  • Green onions
  • Brown sugar
  • Hoisin
  • Rice vinegar
  • Ketchup
  • Soy sauce
  • Water
  • Sriracha
  • Cornstarch
  • Sesame oil
  • Rice
  • Tofu
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Cook rice.
  2. Drain and press tofu for about 10 minutes.
  3. Mix sauce ingredients and set aside.
  4. Slice tofu, coat in avocado oil and cornstarch, and bake at 425°F for 20–25 minutes.
  5. Cook broccoli until charred at the edges, then add sesame oil, green onions, and ginger.
  6. Add sauce and tofu, simmer until thickened, and top with sesame seeds.
