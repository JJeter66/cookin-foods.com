General Tso's Tofu

Crispy tofu and broccoli tossed in a sticky, sweet, spicy General Tso-style sauce.

Ingredients

Tofu and Vegetables

  • 1 block tofu
  • 3 heads broccoli
  • 1 stack green onions
  • 3 tbsp fresh grated ginger
  • Canola or avocado oil
  • ⅓ cup cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 cup uncooked rice

Sauce

  • 4 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp hoisin
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tbsp sriracha, or to taste

Garnish

  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

1

Prepare rice and tofu

Cook rice. Drain and press tofu for about 10 minutes.

2

Make sauce

Whisk brown sugar, hoisin, rice vinegar, ketchup, soy sauce, water, and sriracha.

3

Bake tofu

Cut tofu into bite-size pieces, coat lightly with oil and cornstarch, and bake at 425°F for 20–25 minutes.

4

Cook broccoli

Cook broccoli in a hot pan with oil until charred at the edges. Add sesame oil, green onions, and ginger.

5

Finish

Return tofu to the pan, add sauce, and simmer until thickened. Serve over rice with sesame seeds.

Notes

  • Cook the broccoli hard enough to get charred edges before adding sauce.
