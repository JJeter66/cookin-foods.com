Cabbage and Potato Soup

Comforting cabbage, leek, potato, and white bean soup with lemon and thyme.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Butter or olive oil
  • Carrots
  • Leeks
  • Green or Savoy cabbage
  • Russet or yellow potatoes
  • Vegetable broth
  • Garlic
  • Sea salt
  • Cannellini beans
  • Fresh thyme
  • Lemon juice
  • Black pepper
  • Parmesan, optional

Instructions

  1. Sweat leeks in butter or oil until soft and golden.
  2. Add sliced leeks and cook about 10 minutes.
  3. Add cabbage and cook until wilted.
  4. Add garlic, vegetable broth, potatoes, and thyme.
  5. Simmer until potatoes are fork-tender.
  6. Finish with lemon juice, beans, salt, pepper, and optional parmesan.
