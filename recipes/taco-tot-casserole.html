Taco Tot Casserole

Taco-inspired tater tot casserole with beans, impossible beef, tomatoes, cheese, and corn.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Nonstick spray
  • Canola oil
  • Yellow onion
  • Impossible beef
  • Taco seasoning
  • Garlic powder
  • Kosher salt
  • Pinto beans
  • Diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • Frozen corn
  • Sour cream
  • Mexican-blend cheese
  • Frozen potato tots
  • Cilantro

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F and grease a baking dish.
  2. Cook onion and impossible beef with taco seasoning, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Add beans, tomatoes, and corn, then stir in sour cream and cheese.
  4. Transfer to baking dish, top with cheese and frozen potato tots.
  5. Bake 25–30 minutes until tots are browned and filling bubbles.
