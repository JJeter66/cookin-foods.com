Taco Tot Casserole

Taco-inspired tater tot casserole with beans, impossible beef, tomatoes, cheese, and corn.

Ingredients

Casserole

  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • 2 tbsp canola oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 lb Impossible beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • 2 cans pinto beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 package Mexican-blend cheese, divided
  • 1 package frozen potato tots
  • Cilantro, optional

Instructions

1

Prepare oven and dish

Preheat oven to 425°F and grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.

2

Cook beef

Heat oil in a skillet. Cook onion until softened, then add impossible beef. Stir in taco seasoning, garlic powder, and salt.

3

Finish filling

Add beans, tomatoes, and corn. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream and part of the cheese.

4

Assemble

Transfer filling to baking dish. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and arrange frozen tots in a single layer on top.

5

Bake

Bake 25–30 minutes until tots are golden brown and filling bubbles. Garnish with cilantro if desired.

Notes

  • Skip the microwave when reheating; reheat in a 350°F oven or toaster oven for crisp tots.
