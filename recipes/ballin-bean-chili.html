Soups

Ballin' Bean Chili

A hearty bean chili with impossible beef, tomatoes, corn, and warm spices.

Ingredients

Vegetables and Oil

  • 2 tbsp avocado oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 cup frozen corn

Spices

  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cayenne, optional

Cans and Protein

  • 28 oz diced tomatoes
  • 15 oz crushed tomatoes or tomato sauce
  • 15 oz kidney beans
  • 15 oz black beans
  • 15 oz pinto beans
  • 15 oz butter beans
  • Impossible beef

Instructions

1

Cook vegetables

Add oil to a large pot and sauté vegetables with half the salt over medium heat until tender and starting to char.

2

Brown protein

Make a well in the vegetables and cook impossible beef until browned.

3

Season

Add spices, corn, and beans. Cook over medium heat, covered, about 7 minutes.

4

Simmer

Add diced tomatoes and crushed tomatoes. Simmer 10 minutes.

5

Finish

Season with more spice, salt, hot sauce, or cayenne as desired.

Notes

  • Optional Frito pie: layer Fritos, chili, sour cream, hot sauce, and diced onion.
