Ballin' Bean Chili

A hearty bean chili with impossible beef, tomatoes, corn, and warm spices.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Avocado oil
  • Garlic cloves
  • Big onion
  • Bell pepper
  • Corn
  • Cumin
  • Chili powder
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Cayenne
  • Diced tomatoes
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Kidney beans
  • Black beans
  • Pinto beans
  • Butter beans
  • Impossible beef

Instructions

  1. Sauté vegetables with salt until tender and charred.
  2. Create a center well and brown impossible beef.
  3. Add spices, corn, and beans and cook several minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Season to taste and optionally serve Frito pie-style.
