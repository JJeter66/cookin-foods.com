Chili Crisp Tofu & Broccoli

Sticky, savory-sweet tofu and broccoli with chili crisp sauce over rice.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Extra-firm tofu
  • Broccoli
  • Scallions
  • Fresh ginger
  • Garlic
  • Vegetable broth
  • Soy sauce
  • Maple syrup
  • Chili crisp
  • Rice vinegar
  • Sesame oil
  • Cornstarch
  • Neutral oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Sesame seeds
  • Steamed white rice

Instructions

  1. Press tofu 30–45 minutes.
  2. Cook rice and preheat oven to 425°F.
  3. Make sauce with ginger, garlic, broth, soy sauce, maple syrup, chili crisp, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and cornstarch.
  4. Coat tofu with cornstarch and oil, then bake until crisp.
  5. Cook broccoli until charred.
  6. Simmer sauce until thickened, toss with tofu and broccoli, and serve over rice.
