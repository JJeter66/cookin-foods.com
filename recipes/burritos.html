Burritos

Tofu, bean, rice, salsa, and avocado burritos with Mexican-style baked tofu.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Brown rice
  • Lime juice
  • Cilantro
  • Black beans
  • Pinto beans
  • Yellow onion
  • Olive oil
  • Taco seasoning
  • Water
  • Tortillas
  • Salsa
  • Avocado
  • Tofu
  • Cornstarch
  • Salt
  • Chili powder
  • Cumin
  • Garlic powder
  • Chipotle powder

Instructions

  1. Cook rice, then fluff with lime and cilantro.
  2. Drain and dry tofu.
  3. Coat tofu with cornstarch, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, chipotle powder, salt, and oil.
  4. Bake tofu at 425°F for about 25 minutes.
  5. Cook onion, beans, water, and taco seasoning.
  6. Fill warm tortillas with rice, beans, tofu, salsa, and avocado.
