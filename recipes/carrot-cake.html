Carrot Cake

A classic carrot cake with walnuts, pineapple, warm spices, and cream cheese frosting.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/2 cup crushed pineapple
  • Cream cheese frosting

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease two 9-inch round pans.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients together.
  3. Beat eggs, sugars, oil, and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients, then add carrots, walnuts, and pineapple.
  5. Bake 30–35 minutes, cool, frost, and chill before serving.
