Holiday Stuffing

Savory vegetarian holiday stuffing with mushrooms, herbs, bread, broth, and cream.

Ingredients

Bread Base

  • 1 lb day-old bread, cubed

Savory Backbone

  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 10–12 oz mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced

Herbs and Seasoning

  • 2 tsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1½ tsp fresh thyme
  • 1 tsp fresh rosemary, minced
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¾–1 tsp kosher salt

Liquid

  • 2–2½ cups warm vegetable stock
  • ¼ cup heavy cream, optional
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce or vegetarian Worcestershire

Optional Boosters

  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Pinch white pepper or cayenne

Instructions

1

Dry bread

Spread bread cubes on baking sheets and bake at 300°F for 20–30 minutes until dry but not browned.

2

Build flavor base

Melt butter over medium heat. Cook onion and celery slowly until very soft. Add mushrooms and cook until moisture evaporates and they begin to brown. Add garlic and herbs for 30 seconds.

3

Assemble

Combine dried bread and mushroom mixture in a large bowl. Stir in parmesan or nutritional yeast if using. Whisk stock, cream, soy sauce, and Worcestershire. Pour gradually over bread, tossing gently.

4

Bake

Transfer to a buttered dish. Cover and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake another 20–25 minutes until crisp and golden.

Notes

  • You want stuffing moist, not soupy. Crisp uncovered at the end.
