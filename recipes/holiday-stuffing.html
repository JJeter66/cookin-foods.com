Holiday Stuffing

Savory vegetarian holiday stuffing with mushrooms, herbs, bread, broth, and cream.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Day-old bread, cubed
  • Butter
  • Yellow onion
  • Celery
  • Mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Sage
  • Thyme
  • Rosemary
  • Black pepper
  • Kosher salt
  • Vegetable stock
  • Heavy cream
  • Soy sauce or vegetarian Worcestershire
  • Parmesan, optional

Instructions

  1. Dry bread cubes at 300°F for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Cook onion and celery slowly in butter until soft.
  3. Add mushrooms, garlic, and herbs.
  4. Combine bread with mushroom mixture, parmesan, and liquid gradually.
  5. Bake covered at 375°F for 25 minutes, then uncovered 20–25 minutes.
