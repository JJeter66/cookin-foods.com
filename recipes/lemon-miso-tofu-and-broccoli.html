Lemon Miso Tofu and Broccoli

Crispy baked tofu and charred broccoli in a lemon-miso sauce over rice.

Ingredients

Tofu and Broccoli

  • 1 block extra-firm tofu, drained, dried, and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • ⅓ cup cornstarch
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Neutral oil
  • 2 heads broccoli, chopped
  • 6 scallions, thinly sliced
  • Cooked rice

Lemon-Miso Sauce

  • ¾ cup just-boiled water
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp salt

Finishers

  • 1-inch piece ginger, peeled and grated
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely grated

Instructions

1

Heat oven

Preheat oven to 425°F.

2

Prep tofu

Pat tofu dry, toss with cornstarch, salt, pepper, and oil, then spread on a lined baking sheet. Bake 20–25 minutes, flipping once.

3

Char broccoli

Cook broccoli in a hot pan with oil and salt until charred but not steamed.

4

Make sauce

Whisk boiling water, miso, lemon juice, maple syrup, cornstarch, and salt until smooth.

5

Finish

Cook sesame oil, garlic, and ginger briefly. Add sauce and simmer until glossy. Toss in tofu and broccoli.

6

Serve

Serve immediately over rice with scallions.

Notes

  • If tofu is not crisp, bake it longer. If the sauce is too thick, splash in hot water.
