Lemon Miso Tofu and Broccoli

Crispy baked tofu and charred broccoli in a lemon-miso sauce over rice.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Extra-firm tofu
  • Cornstarch
  • Salt and pepper
  • Neutral oil
  • Broccoli
  • Scallions
  • Cooked rice
  • Boiling water
  • White miso paste
  • Fresh lemon juice
  • Maple syrup
  • Fresh ginger
  • Toasted sesame oil
  • Garlic

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Pat tofu dry, coat with cornstarch, salt, pepper, and oil, then bake until crisp.
  3. Char broccoli in a hot pan.
  4. Whisk miso, lemon juice, maple syrup, cornstarch, salt, and water.
  5. Cook garlic, ginger, and sesame oil, then add sauce until glossy.
  6. Toss tofu and broccoli in sauce and serve over rice.
