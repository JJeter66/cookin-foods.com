Tex-Mex Style Queso

A silky Tex-Mex queso with jalapeños, tomatoes, American cheese, and warm spices.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 fresh jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp pickled serrano chiles, optional
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 13 oz evaporated milk
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 lb yellow American cheese, grated
  • 1/2 lb white American cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1 can green chiles
  • Fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion, jalapeños, and garlic in butter until softened.
  2. Add serrano chiles and tomatoes and cook until tomatoes soften.
  3. Whisk cornstarch into evaporated milk, then add water.
  4. Pour into skillet and stir until the base begins to thicken.
  5. Add cheeses and stir until smooth.
  6. Season with cumin, onion powder, pepper, salt, green chiles, and cilantro.
