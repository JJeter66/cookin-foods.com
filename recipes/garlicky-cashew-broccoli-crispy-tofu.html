Garlicky Cashew Broccoli with Crispy Tofu

Crispy tofu, broccoli, cashews, and couscous in a garlicky sauce.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Whole wheat couscous
  • Vegetable broth
  • Soy sauce
  • Extra-firm tofu
  • Cornstarch
  • Garlic powder
  • Oil
  • Brown sugar
  • Hot water
  • Lemon juice
  • Vegan butter
  • Broccoli florets
  • Cashews
  • Garlic
  • Sriracha
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Prepare couscous with broth and soy sauce.
  2. Coat tofu with cornstarch, salt, and garlic powder, then fry until crisp.
  3. Dissolve brown sugar in hot water, add soy sauce and lemon juice.
  4. Cook broccoli, then add cashews and garlic.
  5. Return tofu to pan with sauce and toss until coated.
  6. Serve over couscous with sesame seeds and sriracha.
