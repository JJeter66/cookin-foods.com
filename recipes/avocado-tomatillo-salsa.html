Avocado Tomatillo Salsa

Creamy tomatillo-avocado salsa for chips, tacos, bowls, and burritos.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb fresh tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 2 lb avocados, ripe, peeled, and pitted
  • 1 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1 white onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water or reserved tomatillo cooking liquid

Instructions

  1. Boil tomatillos 8–10 minutes or roast at 425°F for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Add tomatillos, avocado, cilantro, onion, garlic, lime juice, and salt to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth, thinning with water or reserved tomatillo liquid as needed.
  4. Taste and adjust lime and salt.
