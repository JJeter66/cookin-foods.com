Lemony Dijon Shallot Veggies with Tofu

Roasted tofu, potatoes, and broccoli with a punchy lemon-Dijon shallot vinaigrette.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 1.25 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 2.5 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 fat shallots
  • Fresh rosemary
  • Fingerling potatoes
  • Broccoli
  • Agave
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 blocks tofu

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Cut broccoli, potatoes, and tofu.
  3. Whisk lemon juice, Dijon, agave, salt, shallots, rosemary, and olive oil into a vinaigrette.
  4. Coat tofu, potatoes, and broccoli with vinaigrette on separate pans.
  5. Roast tofu on top rack and broccoli below, flipping halfway through.
  6. Serve with extra lemon juice and salt.
