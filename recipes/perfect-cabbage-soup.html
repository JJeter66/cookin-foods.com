The Perfect Cabbage Soup

A bright cabbage soup with white beans, tomatoes, lemon, herbs, and vegetable broth.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Canola oil
  • Lemon juice
  • Onion
  • Carrot
  • Celery
  • Garlic
  • Cabbage
  • Parsley
  • Oregano
  • Basil
  • Salt
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • Cannellini or white beans
  • Veggie broth

Instructions

  1. Sauté onions, carrot, and celery until softened.
  2. Add garlic and spices.
  3. Add cabbage and sweat 5–10 minutes.
  4. Pour in broth, tomatoes, and beans, then simmer uncovered.
  5. Finish with lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper.
