The Perfect Cabbage Soup

A bright cabbage soup with white beans, tomatoes, lemon, herbs, and vegetable broth.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 2 tbsp canola oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 large carrot, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ½ cabbage, chopped
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 14 oz can chopped tomatoes
  • 14 oz can lima beans or other white beans
  • 4 cups vegetable broth

Instructions

1

Sauté vegetables

Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add diced onion, carrot, and celery and cook about 5 minutes.

2

Add aromatics

Add minced garlic and spices and stir for about 1 minute.

3

Sweat cabbage

Add cabbage and let it sweat 5–10 minutes, stirring frequently.

4

Simmer

Pour in vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, and beans. Simmer uncovered about 10 minutes, or until vegetables are softened to your liking.

5

Finish

Remove from heat. Stir in lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper to taste.

Notes

  • Lemon at the end keeps the soup bright.
