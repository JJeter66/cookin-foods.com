Green Bean Casserole

A rich green bean casserole with creamy mushroom sauce and extra-crunchy onions.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb green beans
  • Butter
  • Sweet onion
  • Cremini mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Flour
  • Whole milk
  • Heavy cream
  • Sour cream
  • Soy sauce or vegetarian Worcestershire
  • Paprika
  • Black pepper
  • Kosher salt
  • Large sweet onions
  • Buttermilk
  • Cornstarch
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Soak sliced onions in buttermilk, dredge with flour mixture, and fry until crispy.
  2. Boil green beans until just tender, then shock and dry.
  3. Cook onion and mushrooms in butter until golden.
  4. Add garlic, flour, milk, cream, sour cream, soy sauce, and seasonings.
  5. Toss green beans with sauce, fold in half the onions, top with remaining onions, and bake uncovered 25–30 minutes.
