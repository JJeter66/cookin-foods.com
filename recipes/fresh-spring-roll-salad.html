Fresh Spring Roll Salad

A fresh spring roll-inspired salad with baked tofu, cold noodles, herbs, vegetables, and peanut sauce.

Ingredients

Baked Tofu

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves, pressed or minced

Salad

  • Rice noodles, cooked and rinsed cold
  • Green onions, chopped
  • Cilantro, chopped
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • ½ cucumber, sliced
  • Romaine, spring mix, or baby spinach

Peanut Sauce

  • ⅓ cup peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup or agave
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2–3 garlic cloves, pressed or minced

Recommended Sauces

  • House of Tsang Classic Sauce
  • House of Tsang Szechuan Spicy
  • Mai Ploy Sweet Chili Sauce
  • Sriracha

Instructions

1

Prepare the noodles and tofu

Preheat the oven to 425°F. Start the rice noodles according to package directions, then rinse them under cold water and drain well.

2

Press and marinate the tofu

Drain the tofu, wrap it in paper towels, and press while you make the marinade. Mix olive oil, soy sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar, and garlic. Cut tofu into bite-size pieces and marinate 5–10 minutes.

3

Bake the tofu

Spread tofu on a lined sheet pan and bake 20–25 minutes, flipping once if desired, until the edges are browned and firm.

4

Make the peanut sauce

Whisk peanut butter, rice vinegar, soy sauce, maple syrup, toasted sesame oil, and garlic until smooth. Thin with a small splash of water if needed.

5

Assemble

Add greens, noodles, vegetables, tofu, and peanut sauce to bowls. Top with any recommended sauces you like.

Notes

  • For spring rolls instead of salad, soak rice paper briefly in water and roll the same fillings inside.
