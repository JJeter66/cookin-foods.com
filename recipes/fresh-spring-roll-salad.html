Fresh Spring Roll Salad

A fresh, crunchy spring roll-inspired salad with baked tofu, herbs, noodles, and peanut sauce.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • Green onions
  • Cilantro
  • 2 fat avocados
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • Romaine, spring mix, or baby spinach
  • Peanut butter
  • Toasted sesame oil
  • Rice noodles

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F and start noodles according to package directions.
  2. Drain tofu, wrap in paper towels, and press while prepping sauce.
  3. Mix tofu marinade with olive oil, soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, and garlic.
  4. Slice tofu into bite-sized pieces and marinate 5–10 minutes.
  5. Bake tofu 20–25 minutes, flipping once if desired.
  6. Whisk peanut sauce together until smooth.
  7. Rinse noodles cold, chop vegetables, and assemble bowls with tofu, noodles, vegetables, peanut sauce, and optional chili sauce.
