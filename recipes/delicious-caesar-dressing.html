Delicious Caesar Dressing

A creamy vegetarian Caesar-style dressing with cashews, capers, lemon, garlic, and Dijon.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 cup cashews, soaked
  • 2 tsp vegetarian Worcestershire
  • 2 tsp capers

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender and blend on high until smooth.
  2. Add water if needed to loosen the texture.
  3. Taste and adjust salt, acid, or thickness.
  4. Toss over greens and serve.
