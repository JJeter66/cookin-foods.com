Delicious Caesar Dressing

A creamy vegetarian Caesar-style dressing with cashews, capers, lemon, garlic, and Dijon.

Ingredients

Base

  • ½ cup cashews, soaked about 1 hour
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp vegetarian Worcestershire
  • 2 tsp capers

Seasoning

  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Instructions

1

Blend

Add all ingredients to a blender and blend on high until smooth.

2

Adjust

If the dressing is too thick, add water 1 tbsp at a time until it reaches the texture you want.

3

Season and serve

Taste and adjust salt, lemon, or pepper. Toss with greens or use as a dip.

Notes

  • Soaked cashews make the dressing smoother.
