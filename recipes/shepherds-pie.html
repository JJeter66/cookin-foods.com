Shepherd's Pie

Vegetarian shepherd’s pie with savory mushroom-beef filling and mashed potato topping.

Recovered recipe archiveVegetarian-friendlyGitHub Pages ready

Ingredients

  • Avocado oil
  • 4 russet potatoes
  • 3 carrots
  • 3 celery stalks
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 4 oz mushrooms
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • Fresh sage and thyme
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Tomato paste
  • Flour
  • Veggie broth
  • Red wine
  • Impossible beef

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F and boil potatoes until soft.
  2. Sauté carrots, celery, and onion until softened.
  3. Add mushrooms and garlic, then tomato paste, flour, herbs, broth, wine, and impossible beef.
  4. Mash potatoes with milk and butter.
  5. Transfer filling to baking dish and spread mashed potatoes on top.
  6. Bake 10–15 minutes until top is crisp.
